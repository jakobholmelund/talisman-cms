# Commerce production readiness review

Reviewed on 25 September 2026 against the Talisman CMS integration on Cloudflare Workers and D1, and updated on 26 September 2026 for the incentive and abuse rules, for the store currency, delivery countries, shipping rates and tax, and for the order operations: fulfillment, refunds and disputes, reconciliation, order emails and gift card delivery.

## Decision

The plugin is suitable for a controlled production deployment with public checkout **disabled**. It is **not ready to accept live orders**. The technical payment safeguards below passed local integration tests, but live payments with the store's currency, delivery, shipping and tax settings, operational fulfillment, order emails, and a real catalog still need end-to-end verification.

## Safeguards checked

| Area | Evidence and current behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Account ownership | A paid order or a used sign-in link may create or link a shopper account, but the cart cookie cannot activate it. Asking for a link creates no account; the link row keeps only the token hash and the address. A one-time emailed link is consumed before the account is created or linked and a shopper session is issued, and only then is the account linked to the shared CMS user for that email. A link is sent only to a bare address, never to one with a display name. First-order promotions and referrals depend on purchase history, not on whether an account exists. A new user gets the `customer` role, which has no CMS access. Migration `0016` revokes earlier sessions on unverified accounts. |
| Checkout integrity | D1 transactions reserve stock, credit, promotions, and gift card tender with the pending order. Stripe completion must match the stored session, amount, currency, and payment status. Duplicate events do not create a second payment. Stripe sessions are created in the order currency with Adaptive Pricing off, so Stripe never presents another currency, and they charge shipping and exclusive tax as lines of their own. The redemption guards reserve a code or gift card only for an order whose items, shipping and exclusive tax equal its discount, credit, gift card and charge. Quoting and checkout read a basket's catalog in one batch and reserve stock with one statement per stock table. Every stock write moves the row's concurrency token, so an admin stock save loaded before a reservation gets HTTP 409, and the product editor saves an option value with its stock in one batch. |
| Store settings | The currency, delivery countries, shipping rates and tax mode are Worker settings, checked strictly. An invalid value closes checkout, the discount preview and gift card sales with HTTP 503 before any stock, code, credit or card is reserved, and logs the setting; shoppers see a generic message. Checkout accepts only officially assigned country codes and, when the basket ships, a country on the delivery list that a shipping rate serves. Credit, discounts and split gift card payments leave Stripe's minimum charge for the currency. Gift cards are refused in a store whose currency is not USD. |
| Tax | With a Stripe tax mode, Stripe Tax calculates the tax before the basket is locked, on the items after the discount and credit and on the shipping, for the shipping address or, when nothing ships, the billing address. The order records the calculation, its transaction is recorded after payment, and each refund is mirrored by a partial reversal, recorded before Stripe is asked so no refund is reversed twice. A failure to record or reverse never fails a payment confirmation or webhook; it is logged and the scheduled `reconcileCommerce` retries it, and a lost dispute reverses the tax like a full refund. A failed calculation stops checkout before anything is reserved. Simulated `admin_test` orders are not taxed. |
| Recovery | The order status route (at most one provider check per order every 15 seconds, shared with checkout resume and session release, once the order is 60 seconds old) and a ten-minute scheduled Worker reconcile pending Stripe sessions when a webhook is missed. Orphaned preparation locks are released after the configured Stripe session lifetime; gift card purchases are also reconciled. Each attempt is recorded, and a row waits 2^attempts minutes, at most six hours, before the next one, so rows that keep failing never block newer ones; the tax passes back off the same way. A permanent failure (a session Stripe no longer has, a session of the other Stripe mode, a completed payment that does not match) parks the row for an administrator and is reported once. An administrator retries or releases a parked row with a recorded reason; a completed checkout is released only once Stripe reports its payment refunded in full or lost to a dispute. |
| Fulfillment | Migration `0026` keeps fulfillment apart from the payment status. A paid or partially refunded real order can ship in one or several parcels, and a refund never hides a shipment. Carrier and tracking corrections are appended with the administrator and a reason, keeping the shipment as first recorded. Pending, cancelled, refunded, disputed and admin test orders cannot ship. The admin queue lists every order awaiting shipment, oldest first, with a server count. |
| Refunds and disputes | Stripe events that name none of the store's records are answered HTTP 200 and change nothing, and events for a record they cannot apply to are logged and answered 200 with a reason; a refund or dispute that arrives before its payment is recorded is answered 409, so Stripe retries it, and other failures 500. Each rise in an order's refund total is recorded with its date. A dispute holds its order as `disputed`, which cannot ship, and keeps its referral award pending; a lost dispute refunds the order in full, gift card and store credit tender included, and reverses its tax. A disputed gift card purchase suspends its cards, and a lost dispute voids their unspent value. Restock is manual and audited, once per reservation row. |
| Order emails | Order confirmations, shipment notices and gift card claim emails are recorded in the batch of the payment, shipment or purchase that calls for them, one per event, sent at once and retried by the scheduled job with backoff; a failed send never fails a payment, shipment or webhook. Delivery rows, results and logs hold ids and error codes, never an address. |
| Gift card delivery | The buyer is emailed a one-time link, valid for 7 days, that shows the code once; only the link's hash is stored, and claims are rate-limited per network. An audited resend never stops a working link unless its own email was sent. A refund holds the purchase for an administrator's audited reinstate or void decision, and a replacement card takes over the value left and voids the old card. Codes carry a key id and are bound to their card, and previous keys stay readable during a key rotation. |
| Access | Commerce admin routes require the CMS administrator role and same-origin mutations. Restocks, gift card review decisions, claim link resends and decisions on parked checkouts record the administrator and a reason. Customer cookies are HTTP-only and account link tokens are stored as hashes. An order's status and cancellation are available only to the basket or signed-in account that placed it. The simulated `admin_test` payment provider exists only on sites that set `ecommercePlugin({ adminTestCheckout: true })`, and only the admin route registers it. |
| Basket abuse | The public basket API accepts at most 50 lines of 1 to 99 units, rejects bodies over 64 KB, and stores only catalog products and variants; a product with variant groups is accepted, and sold, only as one of its variant values, never at its base price or as a group that has values. A rejected request creates no basket and reading never creates one. `purgeStaleCommerceData`, run from the scheduled Worker, deletes guest baskets idle for 30 days. Creating a basket is limited to 20 per client network (an IPv4 address or an IPv6 /64) per hour, in the cart route and the `addToCart` action; changes to an open basket are not limited, and checkout never creates a basket. |
| Incentives | Referrals are off until an admin enables them or `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_REFERRALS_ENABLED` is `true` with nothing saved. The reward is at most half the minimum first order, and the minimum applies to the subtotal less the promotion discount. Self-referral and repeat-buyer checks compare exact and canonical addresses. Each referrer, counted across equivalent addresses, has a cap per period. Awards stay pending for a hold period (default 30 days) and the scheduled job releases them only when the payment provider reports no dispute. A refund that leaves the order below the qualifying amount, or a lost dispute, voids the referral and reverses released awards. Discount and gift card code checks share a budget per client network and per basket, and every refused code gets one answer. Stripe checkout coupons are single-use, expire with their session and are deleted when the checkout ends unpaid. |
| Sign-in email | Each address, each network and the store per day are limited. Part of the daily budget is reserved for addresses with a verified account or an order, and once the rest is spent every request gets the same answer. With both Turnstile keys set, a request needs a valid Turnstile token, verified with Cloudflare before any counter, link or email. |
| Verification | `pnpm --filter @talisman-cms/plugin-ecommerce test` passed 375 tests. Route tests cover Stripe signature verification (valid, wrong secret, stale timestamp, changed payload and replay), the checkout-disabled gate on the checkout route, checkout action and gift card purchase, order ownership, basket limits, variant choice, sign-in links and their preview, the Turnstile check and the reserved sign-in budget, referral holds, caps and reversals, discount preview limits and refusals, basket creation limits, the order status throttle and single-use Stripe coupons. Tests also cover the store settings and their 503, a store in another currency and the session's presentment currency, delivery countries, shipping rates with discounts, credit and gift cards under the redemption guards, and Stripe Tax calculation, transactions and reversals, including refunds that race. Tests for the order operations cover split shipments, corrections and the orders queue, gift card review decisions, replacement cards and key rotation, batched option saves and checkout queries with their statement counts, stock tokens, webhook answers, disputes, dated refunds and restock, reconciliation backoff, parking and release, and order emails, including a run with the console email provider; the core migration test upgrades a database holding earlier releases' rows through `0030`. The Talisman TypeScript check, Astro production build and a Wrangler deployment dry run passed at the original review. No live payment or remote migration was performed. |

## Launch gates

1. Create the D1 database from the 0.1 migrations (the core's, then the plugin's, from the assembled folder) **before** deploying the Worker, as the [deployment gate](../../RELEASE.md#deployment-gate) describes; every plugin read fails on a database without them. Take a Time Travel bookmark before any later migration and rehearse the restore.
2. Replace draft product concepts with verified, saleable products. Confirm actual prices in the store currency's minor units, component bills of materials, available stock, images, fit and optical claims, and a physical packing sample.
3. Choose the store currency, the countries served, delivery charges and time promises, tax treatment, returns and refund policy, and customer contact channel. Set them before the first order with `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_CURRENCY`, `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_DELIVERY_COUNTRIES`, `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_SHIPPING_RATES` and `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_TAX` (with `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_TAX_CODE` and `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_SHIP_FROM_COUNTRY` as needed), as the [plugin README](README.md#currency-delivery-shipping-and-tax) describes; unset, checkout sells in USD, accepts any officially assigned country code and charges no shipping or tax. Do not change the currency once orders, gift cards, store credit or discount codes exist. Gift cards work only in a USD store. The storefront should offer the shipping options that `shippingOptionsFor` lists for the shipping country, and with a tax mode set it must send a billing address with a country for a basket with nothing to ship.
4. Configure live Stripe keys and a signed webhook for `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.expired`, `charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created` and `charge.dispute.closed`. Before enabling checkout, run one discounted test-mode checkout and confirm that its Stripe coupon `<order id>_discount` has `max_redemptions` 1 and a `redeem_by` equal to the session's expiry, that the session is created without an error on `expires_at`, and that the coupon is deleted after the session expires or the order is cancelled. In the test-mode run, also confirm that a refund's `charge.refunded` event carries `metadata.orderId`, which checkout sets on the payment, so that a refund arriving before its payment is recorded gets HTTP 409 and is retried. Delete unused coupons that earlier versions created (random ids) in the Stripe dashboard. Keep the scheduled Worker calling `reconcileCommerce` with the Stripe adapter: it repairs missed webhooks, records and reverses tax that was not recorded or reversed in time, releases held referral awards and retries order emails. Run a real low-value order through payment and its confirmation email, reconciliation, fulfillment and the shipment notice, refund, and restock before opening checkout broadly.

   Before setting `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_STRIPE_MODE=live`, make sure no test-mode order or gift card purchase is pending, and set the mode and the live keys together: a missing or wrong mode leaves Stripe unconfigured, and reconciliation then backs off with `provider_not_configured`. This read-only query must return no rows; for a switch back to test, use `cs_live_`:

   ```sql
   SELECT 'order' AS kind, id, created_at FROM _ecommerce_orders
     WHERE status = 'pending' AND COALESCE(payment_provider, 'stripe') = 'stripe'
       AND substr(COALESCE(checkout_session_id, ''), 1, 8) = 'cs_test_'
   UNION ALL
   SELECT 'gift_card_purchase', id, created_at FROM _ecommerce_gift_card_purchases
     WHERE status = 'pending' AND substr(COALESCE(provider_session_id, ''), 1, 8) = 'cs_test_';
   ```

   Wait for such rows to expire, or release them, before the switch. A row left over is parked by the first scheduled run after it and reported once, but with the default limit a run parks at most 10 orders and 10 gift card purchases, so a backlog fails several runs in a row: the larger of the two counts divided by 10, rounded up. Parked rows appear under **Payment checks parked for review** on the **Orders** screen and keep their stock and basket locked until an administrator retries or releases them with a reason. Release a session of the other mode after checking it in that mode's Stripe dashboard, where a paid one must be refunded first; release a missing session after checking every Stripe account the store has used; release a completed session that does not match once its payment is refunded in full in Stripe or lost to a dispute, or else fix the cause and retry it. A session of the other mode, or one Stripe no longer has, may still be paid until it expires, so its release is refused until 35 minutes after the checkout started.
5. Configure an email provider (a Cloudflare Email Service `[[send_email]]` binding named `EMAIL` on an onboarded sending domain, or a custom provider), the `TALISMAN_EMAIL_FROM` sender and an https `TALISMAN_PUBLIC_ORIGIN`. Turn off any provider feature that stores message content, because sign-in links are bearer credentials for 15 minutes and gift card claim links for 7 days. Prove that the checkout email receives a one-time sign-in link and an unrelated browser cannot use the cart cookie to enter that account. Review `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_EMAIL_DAILY_LIMIT` (default 200 sign-in emails a day) against the provider's quota, and `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_EMAIL_RESERVED_DAILY` (default a quarter of it) for existing customers; size the quota for order confirmations, shipment notices and claim emails on top of that, since the daily limit counts sign-in emails only. Set the store details and policy links that order emails carry (`TALISMAN_COMMERCE_STORE_NAME`, `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_STORE_LEGAL_NAME`, `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_STORE_ADDRESS`, `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_SUPPORT_EMAIL`, `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_TERMS_URL`, `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_RETURNS_URL` and `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_WARRANTY_URL`), and in a test-mode run check that one order sends one confirmation, a recorded shipment sends its notice, and a gift card purchase sends a claim email whose `/gift-cards/claim` link shows the code once. Have counsel review the email wording and the store details. The default emails are in English, and the confirmation links to the terms, returns and warranty pages rather than carrying their text, which the rules on confirming a contract on a durable medium may require; a store template can add it. If Stripe's own email receipts are on, buyers get those too. Create a Cloudflare Turnstile widget in Managed mode for the storefront's hostname, set `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` and the secret `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY` together, render the widget from `shopperSignInBotCheck(env)`, and check that a sign-in link still arrives and that a request without a token gets HTTP 403.
6. Assign staff to work the admin order queue every day: pack and label parcels and record each shipment (the plugin emails the shipment notice), correct carrier or tracking mistakes, resolve failed deliveries, refund in Stripe and return stock with **Return stock**, respond to disputes in Stripe, and decide the checkouts under **Payment checks parked for review**. Watch the read-only **Order Emails** collection, and alert on the scheduled run's `error` results and on the `[commerce] Stripe event does not match the store records`, `[commerce] Stripe webhook failed` and `[commerce] Email not sent` log lines. Repeated webhook answers of HTTP 409 mean a pending order or purchase that reconciliation has not settled. The plugin does not buy labels.
7. Keep `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_CHECKOUT_ENABLED=false` until these gates pass. Enable gift card sales separately, only in a USD store, and only after their key backup (the current key and every previous one) and their purchase, claim email, admin resend, refund review and replacement procedures are tested. A Stripe refund of a gift card purchase suspends its cards and lists the purchase under **Held for review**, where an administrator reinstates or voids it with a reason; a void cannot be undone and waits for checkouts in progress. To replace a lost card, issue a card that names the purchase, for exactly the value left on it; the old card is voided and its claim links stop working.
8. Add a Cloudflare rate limiting rule, keyed on the client IP, for every public path under `/api/ecommerce/` except `/api/ecommerce/webhooks/`: today `account`, `cart`, `checkout`, `discount`, `gift-cards` and `order`, for every request method, because a `GET` of `order` for a pending order asks the payment provider for its status. Add `/_actions/*` on sites that use the plugin's Astro actions. Leave the Stripe webhook paths out of every per-IP rule: `/api/ecommerce/webhooks/*`, and `/api/stripe/webhooks` with plugin-stripe. Stripe sends webhooks from a small set of addresses, so a per-IP limit would drop deliveries. Check that the zone's plan supports the counting period and block duration you choose; otherwise use the closest values it allows. On a plan that allows a single rate limiting rule, match all these paths in one rule. The rule is defence in depth next to the plugin's own limits: new baskets per network, code checks per network and per basket, sign-in email per address, per network and per day, gift card claims per network, and provider checks per order from the order status route, checkout resume and session release. Requests without a client IP are not counted by the plugin's per-network limits.

9. Decide whether referrals run before opening checkout. They are off unless a saved policy or `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_REFERRALS_ENABLED` enables them; check the saved policy with `SELECT enabled, reward_cents, min_order_cents FROM _ecommerce_referral_settings`. Before enabling them, publish referral terms (reward, minimum first order, hold period, cap per period and reversal on refunds and lost disputes), update the privacy and cookie notices for the referral cookie, and show a referred shopper's own pending welcome award, which `getReferralDashboard` does not yet list.
10. Before setting `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_TAX` to a Stripe mode, activate Stripe Tax in the Stripe Dashboard: set the origin address, add a registration for every country or state where the store must collect tax, and set the default product tax code or `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_TAX_CODE`. Stripe Tax calculates no tax where the account has no registration. Decide with the store's tax adviser whether prices include tax (`stripe-inclusive`) or have it added (`stripe-exclusive`), and set `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_SHIP_FROM_COUNTRY` if the goods ship from a country other than the origin address's.
11. Run a Stripe test-mode checkout with the store settings the launch will use, a test card and a delivery address outside the store's own country (a billing address, for a basket with nothing to ship). Confirm that the payment page shows only the store currency, not a local one; that the session's lines are the items, the shipping rate under its label and, with `stripe-exclusive`, a "Tax" line, all in the store currency; that the session's total equals the order's `total_amount`; and that the order confirms. With tax on, also confirm that the order records `tax_calculation_id` and, after payment, `tax_transaction_id`; that a partial refund and then a full refund each create a partial reversal in Stripe Tax, adding up to the refunded amount; that a calculation is accepted when store credit pays for every item and shipping remains; and that an address Stripe Tax cannot place, such as a US address with an invalid ZIP code, is refused with HTTP 400.
12. Confirm in test mode that a session whose one coupon is larger than its item lines is accepted and charges the order's total: in a USD store, a basket with a shipping charge (and, with `stripe-exclusive`, a Tax line) paid with a gift card that covers more than the items but not the whole total, so the coupon for the discount, credit and gift card together also covers part of the shipping or tax. The admin **Orders** screen should show the same totals.

## Known limits

- The referral minimum in force at checkout is not stored on the order. After an admin raises the minimum, a refund is measured against the lower of the new minimum and what the order qualified with at checkout, not against the old minimum. Storing the minimum on the order needs a migration.
- A Stripe checkout coupon is deleted once, when its order is cancelled. If that delete fails it is not retried, and the coupon remains until its `redeem_by`, single-use. The same holds for the coupon of a checkout of the other Stripe mode that an administrator releases, which this mode's key cannot delete.
- The referral and first-purchase checks compare canonical addresses by scanning purchased orders; an indexed canonical column needs a migration.
- Gift cards and their purchases are kept in USD only (migration `0015`), so a store with another currency cannot issue, sell or redeem them until those tables are rebuilt.
- Store credit, referral terms, discount values and voucher balances carry no currency. Changing `TALISMAN_COMMERCE_CURRENCY` after they exist reads them in the new currency.
- The store currency must be one of `SUPPORTED_CURRENCIES`: currencies with a known Stripe minimum charge whose minor units `Intl` and Stripe agree on. Any other code closes checkout.
- The discount preview has no destination, so its credit and amount to pay leave out shipping and tax and can differ from checkout's.
- Stripe keeps idempotency keys for 24 hours and tax calculations for 90 days. A tax transaction or reversal that Stripe completed but whose id was not stored is refused as a duplicate reference when it is retried more than 24 hours later, and a transaction cannot be recorded once its calculation has expired. Such a record or reversal is not parked: `reconcileCommerce` tries it again after waits that grow to six hours (its `tax_sync_attempts`), without holding back newer ones, and reports an error each time until it is resolved by hand.
- A lost dispute returns the gift card and store credit tender of the order to the shopper, as a full refund does.
- A shipment that completes an order is final, so an order whose first parcel was recorded as the last cannot be reopened. A partial shipment has no idempotency key: a request retried after a lost answer records a second parcel, which cannot be removed. A partly shipped order whose remaining items are refunded instead stays in **Awaiting shipment** until a completing shipment is recorded with a note that explains it.
- Order emails are in English, with dates in UTC. Delivery is at least once: if a Worker stops between the provider's acceptance and its record, the email is sent again after the five-minute lease, and a repeated claim email carries a second working link. Nothing turns order emails off, so a store that takes orders without email configured gets `error` results until the waiting emails expire after 7 days.
- The gift card claim email goes to the buyer address entered at purchase, which is not verified, and its link works for 7 days.
- An order refund that restores gift card value to a card that was replaced credits the void card, where it cannot be spent; issue a card for it by hand.
- Checkout sessions created before this release carry no PaymentIntent metadata, so an early refund or dispute of their payments is ignored instead of retried, and a refund of an order that recorded no payment intent is not applied.
- A pending gift card purchase that never recorded its Stripe session, because its Worker stopped in between, is never reconciled or cleaned up. `listCustomerOrders` shows a parked order as pending without `paymentUnderReview`.

Site deployment steps and secret names belong in each site's own deployment notes; the CMS deployment gate is in [RELEASE.md](../../RELEASE.md#deployment-gate). This review covers the code and local integration; it does not certify the merchant's legal, tax, product, or operational readiness.
